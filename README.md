# CapexQuant SABI Castellón

Reproducible financial and data-quality analysis of a SABI business dataset containing companies located in the province of Castellón, Spain.

## Project objective

The project develops a professional analytical pipeline to:

- clean and validate company-level financial data;
- identify extinct, dissolved and liquidated entities;
- analyze revenue, EBITDA and employment;
- measure business concentration;
- detect financial and accounting anomalies;
- generate reproducible tables, charts and reports;
- document the limitations of heterogeneous corporate data.

## Initial findings

The preliminary analysis identified:

- 6,711 companies in the original extraction;
- 164,781 employees;
- EUR 42.7 billion in aggregated revenue;
- EUR 2.6 billion in aggregated EBITDA;
- 24.5% of companies with available data reporting negative EBITDA;
- 17.4% of records containing an adverse legal-status marker;
- approximately 56.3% of revenue concentrated in the 100 largest companies;
- positive median revenue growth despite a decline in aggregate revenue.

> The financial figures combine each company's latest available financial year. They must not be interpreted as a homogeneous provincial total for a single fiscal year.
